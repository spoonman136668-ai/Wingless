# Wingless UP-181C — stage-two delay boundary

Status: preregistered scientific counterfactual intervention experiment.

Scientific parent: sealed UP-180C 176720ea845a6599c42a45747b941a3679badf06.

## Question

How wide is the useful timing window for the second warning-triggered rescue action?

## Frozen environment

- no_refresh pressure
- cadences 2 and 4
- four cohorts
- all initial hand positions 0..15
- maximum two targeted actions
- action two requires a fresh later warning

## Frozen timing

Stage-one delay is fixed at 0 because UP-180C found no stage-one delay effect.

Stage-two delay is swept:
- 0 monitoring intervals
- 1 monitoring interval
- 2 monitoring intervals

Targets are always the endangered resident.

## Measurements

Per cadence × stage-two delay:
- stage-one actions
- stage-two actions
- eventual losses
- prevented losses
- prevented losses per action
- mean loss delay among treated failures

## Interpretation

This defines the second-stage timing window: immediate, one-interval, or two-interval action. A peak at delay 1 with collapse at delay 2 would identify a bounded temporal sweet spot rather than a monotonic wait benefit.

## Bounds

Counterfactual only. No adaptive delay, target, action budget, warning threshold, policy input, capacity change, live activation, or production authority.
