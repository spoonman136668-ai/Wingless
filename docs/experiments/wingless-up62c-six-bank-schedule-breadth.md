## Question

Are the six-bank golden-rotation and fixed-irregular robustness observations stable across a broader untouched schedule set at two already meaningful noise levels?

## Frozen design

Families:

- golden rotation;
- fixed irregular.

Untouched deterministic schedule bases:

- 79M;
- 80M;
- 81M;
- 82M;
- 83M;
- 84M.

Noise levels:

- 0.0075;
- 0.0100.

All other conditions are unchanged: six banks, state dimension 16, 256 scenarios, depth 64, coherence decoder, reversible transport, and the existing 0.99 value / 0.95 exact-scenario gate.

No geometry modification, decoder change, threshold change, family selection, result-informed stopping, production authority, or activation is permitted.

## Interpretation

This is schedule-breadth replication only. Schedule sensitivity or failure is a valid scientific result and does not itself authorize promotion.
