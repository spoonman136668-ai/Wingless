## Question

UP-61C bounded the golden-rotation transition between 0.010 and 0.011 noise and the fixed-irregular transition between 0.0085 and 0.009 on two untouched schedules.

UP-63C asks whether those exact boundary intervals replicate across a wider untouched schedule set.

## Frozen design

Untouched deterministic schedule bases:

- 85M;
- 86M;
- 87M;
- 88M;
- 89M;
- 90M.

Golden-rotation noise levels:

- 0.010;
- 0.011.

Fixed-irregular noise levels:

- 0.0085;
- 0.0090.

All other conditions are unchanged: six banks, state dimension 16, 256 scenarios, depth 64, coherence decoder, reversible transport, and the existing 0.99 value / 0.95 exact-scenario gate.

No geometry modification, decoder change, threshold change, family selection, adaptive stopping, production authority, or activation is permitted.

## Interpretation

This is boundary replication only. Schedule-dependent transitions are valid scientific results and will be sealed exactly as observed. No mechanism promotion is authorized.
