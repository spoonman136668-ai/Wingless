# Wingless UP-57C — confirmed golden phase-code bank scaling

Status: preregistered scientific scale experiment.

Scientific parent: UP-56C seal `8c61fb93f7764367ef6a17405431756c9537a0f3`.

UP-56C independently confirmed four-bank golden-angle multiplexing through noise 0.04. UP-57C asks whether the same construction extends simultaneous memory capacity without increasing the 16-dimensional state.

Two untouched deterministic schedules are used. Frozen conditions are: four banks at noise 0.04 as a confirmation control; five banks at noise 0, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, and 0.04; and six banks at zero noise to test exact representational separability. Golden tags are generated by the same fixed golden-angle increment for every bank.

No new tag selection, dimension increase, decoder change, or threshold change occurs.
