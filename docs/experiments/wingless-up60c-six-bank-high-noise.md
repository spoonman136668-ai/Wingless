Status: preregistered scientific scale experiment.

Scientific parent: UP-59C seal `55f1725554d4f7493f206852385545c74c2d5d69`.

## Question

UP-59C showed that golden and fixed-irregular six-bank codes remain perfect through noise 0.0075 on two untouched schedules, while the quadratic code crossed the frozen gate between 0.0035 and 0.0045.

UP-60C continues the boundary search for the two still-qualified geometries.

## Frozen design

Families:

- golden rotation;
- fixed irregular.

Untouched schedule bases: 75M and 76M.

Noise ladder: 0.01, 0.015, 0.02, 0.03, 0.04.

All other conditions are unchanged from UP-59C: six banks, state dimension 16, 256 scenarios, depth 64, coherence decoder, reversible transport, and the existing 0.99 value / 0.95 exact-scenario gate.

No geometry modification, threshold change, decoder change, or family selection occurs within this experiment.

## Interpretation

This is boundary continuation for the two surviving frozen geometries. A failure is a valid capacity/robustness result and will be sealed exactly as observed.
