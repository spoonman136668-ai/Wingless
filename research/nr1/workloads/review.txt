Review the following Go service design for correctness and operational risk. The service accepts jobs over HTTP, writes each job to SQLite, and a worker polls for pending jobs. The current proposal marks a job RUNNING in one transaction, commits, performs the external side effect, then marks it DONE in a second transaction. Identify concrete failure modes involving crashes, duplicate execution, stale leases, cancellation, retry accounting, and process restart. For each issue, distinguish correctness defects from observability gaps, propose the smallest repair, and list the deterministic tests that would prove the repair without weakening existing assertions.
