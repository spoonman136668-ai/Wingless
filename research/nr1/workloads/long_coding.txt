Design and implement, in one response, a Go package for a bounded durable work queue backed by SQLite. Required behavior: stable caller-supplied job IDs; idempotent enqueue; FIFO within equal priority; atomic lease acquisition; configurable lease expiry; deterministic recovery of expired leases; bounded retries with immutable attempt history; cancellation that cannot revive a terminal job; crash-safe state transitions; and read-only inspection APIs. Provide the schema, core types, transaction boundaries, enqueue/claim/complete/fail/cancel/recover methods, and table-driven tests for duplicate enqueue, competing workers, crash after claim, lease expiry, retry exhaustion, cancellation races, restart recovery, and FIFO ordering. Keep the design self-contained, avoid external services, and explain any SQLite locking assumptions that affect correctness.
