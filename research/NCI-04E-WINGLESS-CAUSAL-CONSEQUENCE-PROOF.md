**Status:** DEVELOPMENT FEASIBILITY SPECIFICATION / no admitted candidate; no source-first-use.

## Why NCI-04D3 cannot solve the North Star

The opt-in D3 `SourceReasoner.ObserveExistingCall` learns solely from ICE's syntax-call witnesses, and `PredictCall` independently returns `VerifiedSyntaxCall` from that same index. Predicting that witness is a deterministic restatement of already available exact graph information. An exact-graph control has zero loss **by definition** for this target; no learner can improve on it. Persistent snapshots and successful source-index regression do not change the missing information.

**Stop:** Do not tune prototype log-odds, the 16-entity identity matcher, fixed 4D source hashing, or source-call synthetic queries. Preserve the recorded scientific NO-GO. The proven integration code remains quarantined in its unmerged draft.

## Distinct native candidate family: intervention-conditioned causal abstraction

The target signal must be an independently observed consequence of an actual change, not a static source fact:

1. **Action:** isolated, reproducible source edit (exact before/after commits, changed declarations and environment identity).
2. **Consequence:** measured change in a specific real test/behavior outcome under equivalent toolchains and conditions, including negative/no-effect observations and repeats.
3. **Internal cognition:** predictive state groups interventions by their *future behavioral effect*, not lexical similarity or source symbol names. A prediction discrepancy induces structural revision of a previously learned equivalence; related prior knowledge survives unrelated tasks and process restarts.
4. **Reuse:** an intervention seen in one subsystem informs a different source area with analogous behavioral consequences, without being given a symbolic role correspondence. Learned predictions must lead to a measurable improvement in *selecting useful analyses or tests* beyond exact ICE.
5. **Uncertainty:** causal attribution is invalid when a commit bundles unrelated changes, test nondeterminism, environment drift or non-executed branches. Admit observational association as such; do not call it a causal intervention.

This is a research hypothesis, not a claim of novelty. Predictive-state representations and successor-feature/state-abstraction models are important existing comparators. New source files or a more complicated recurrent module do not themselves constitute a new learned mechanism.

## Development-only data admission

There is **no qualified paired intervention/outcome dataset** in the NCI-04D3 evidence. Ordinary single-commit successful GitHub Actions runs are not paired experimental controls, and compiler/test outcomes cannot automatically be assigned to a particular changed symbol.

Before implementing a predictor, enumerate real paired experiments with:
- Exact source predecessor/derived SHAs and source SHA256 for the changed declaration; single-delta first, with isolation of any unrelated changes;
- Frozen test identity, CLI, toolchain/build flags, corpus, environment and independent control/intervention execution receipts;
- Per-test outcome and bounded repeat/no-effect negatives with first-use retention;
- No event selection based on hindsight or on the candidate's own prediction;
- No tracing or deployment into CKB-plane, production trading or unrelated projects.

If these observations cannot be gathered from existing qualified runners without new services or paid APIs, **declare the learning hypothesis not yet testable**. Do not substitute ICE lexical labels or artificial source-call fixtures.

## Feasibility and competitive acceptance

Frozen pre-experiment questions:
- Can retained state predict *unknown change consequences*, across real source-task switches, and restore the state across processes without re-reading hidden outcomes?
- Is reusable causal structure learned from intervention outcomes rather than copied from an exact graph?
- Do counterfactual ablations (delete learned partitions, shuffle action-history alignment, sever learned causal links, remove intervention outcomes) remove the gain?
- Is the gain reproducible on genuinely unrelated source changes under identical information against exact ICE dependency/call graph, per-source and per-test counts, strongest symbolic impact analysis, and fully trained small recurrent/state-space predictors?
- Are total training+inference+test-dispatch CPU, peak RSS, persistent/static weights/indices and snapshot bytes **no higher** than the strongest resource-matched comparator? Include the cost of obtaining extra observations.
- Are hard unsupported impact claims and cross-version knowledge contamination zero?

**NO-GO:** no independent effect observations; any fail-closed source drift; no gain over exact/strong conventional controls; additional compute/memory; unqualified new outcomes. Allow only one frozen discriminating test, not endless hyperparameter tuning.
