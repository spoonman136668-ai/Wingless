# Wingless — active research allocation, 2026-10-14

**Status:** Research-direction reallocation adopted by the operator. Planning and scientific priority only; no production, accepted-reference, controller or experimental-execution authority.

## Allocation (target effort, NOT observed compute)
- **60% foundational discovery:** distinguishable trainable representations, variable binding, state dynamics, memory generalization and falsification.
- **30% native cognition integration:** combine native learned input/sequence state, retained memory, prediction and multi-step readout; evaluate small language-like relations, delayed answers and continual learning.
- **10% research optimization:** prioritize gain per resource, diversity and nonduplicate selection; keep provenance and cost custody.

For a ten-credit *planned effort* tranche use **six foundational, three integration, one optimization** credits. Credits denote scoped research effort, NOT equal-duration runs, equal model FLOPs or proven spend. Track actual runner minutes, development/heldout executions, training operations, memory/weight bytes, outcomes and blocked gates; compare the observed mix to targets after each tranche. Equal scientific opportunity with Yggdrasil; no shared sealed identities or branches.

## Scientific reason for the change
NCI-08 falsified complete future-query retention under seven uncompressed edges. NCI-09 coded compression improved finite synthetic recall, but the compression policy was hand-written. NCI-10 and R2 showed domain-shift failures despite some utility-selected gains. NCI-11 through R3 tested native learned associative/recurrent state but did not deliver qualified unseen-structure transfer. In the latest NCI-11 R3 development check, trained analytic-gradient encoders underperformed the same architectures at initialization; the programmed control remained stronger. These are real negative findings, not evidence to expand the same heuristic sweep.

## Six foundational work credits, in priority order
1. **F1 — Permutation-equivariant variable binding:** train role/filler relationships under randomly renamed entities and counterfactual same-query graphs. Compare with a no-variable-binding model and exact permutation controls. The representation must survive unseen naming and graph motifs.
2. **F2 — Learnable fixed-size state formation from raw observations and history:** jointly fit state update and predictive readout, with matched resident bytes and same-model initialized/zero-state ablations. Stop if trained weights do not materially improve unseen validation.
3. **F3 — Content-addressed retrieval and relation composition:** compare learned sparse addressing, object-centric slots and explicitly coded traversal as separate hypotheses. Report retrieval operations and false positives, not just pooled accuracy.
4. **F4 — Information bottleneck under real byte scarcity:** test incompatible future questions, overload, eviction, checkpoint restore, and negative/unknown answers. Account for state storage, metadata and model weights separately.
5. **F5 — Continual learning and interference:** teach new entity/relation families after initial tasks; measure old accuracy, acquisition, forgetting and recovery without sealed-answer leakage.
6. **F6 — Independent mechanism falsification:** examine whether apparent gains vanish after role swap, entity renaming, relation reversal or a trainable-memory ablation. Archive all negatives and cost regressions.

**Next immediate engineering/science gate:** F1/F2 development-only comparison with at least three mechanistically distinct candidates and matched strong controls; do not reserve a new sealed cohort until a frozen development threshold is met. Do not replay NCI-08 through NCI-11 originals.

## Three integration work credits
1. **I1:** connect native learned observation/state formation to stored learned relationships and compositional answer generation; distinguish training-time labels from runtime available observations.
2. **I2:** evaluate tiny native language-based relationship questions with unseen recombinations (no external LLM answering for Wingless); include false and impossible questions.
3. **I3:** demonstrate delayed context, checkpoints, interruptions and sequential knowledge acquisition while retaining old capabilities.

Integration work continues alongside F1–F6 rather than being paused; it may not be called full NCI-01 success without sealed evidence.

## One optimization work credit
**O1:** score candidate families for novelty, causal clarity, gain per runner minute, resident bytes, training/inference work and heldout scarcity; cache development-only tests by source identity, use one full regression, and prohibit duplicate once-only first-use.
