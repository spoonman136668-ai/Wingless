TITLE: Wingless North Star
DATE: 2026-10-02
STATUS: ACTIVE
TRACK: standalone LLM-class cognitive system

PROGRAM OBJECTIVE
Develop and train the existing Wingless cognitive substrate into a standalone, general-purpose LLM-class system whose own learned state provides language understanding and generation, broad learned knowledge, reasoning, instruction following, multi-turn conversation, persistent cognition, continual learning, self-monitoring, and bounded self-maintenance.

The deployed cognitive path must not require Claude, ChatGPT, or another external frontier model at inference time.

SUBSTRATE PRESERVATION INVARIANT
The established Wingless substrate and its accepted scientific lineage are the architectural foundation. Do not replace them with a conventional transformer or other standard LLM architecture merely to obtain language capability. Language acquisition, representation learning, generation, knowledge acquisition, and general reasoning must emerge from or integrate natively with this substrate.

Conventional LLM architectures may be used as comparison baselines, teachers, data/evaluation tools, or bounded component sources. They are not automatic replacements. Any substrate-changing extension must be preregistered, experimentally justified, bounded to the minimum necessary delta, preserve previously established capabilities unless the experiment explicitly tests them, and qualify affected interfaces before acceptance.

NATIVE CAPABILITY REQUIREMENT
A capability counts toward the North Star only when it is produced by the Wingless/Yggdrasil system's own learned state and substrate. External-model assistance during research or training does not count as model capability, and an external frontier model must not be required for deployed inference.

LLM-CLASS CAPABILITY AXES
- native representation and sequence/language learning from training data;
- generative prediction and coherent free-form text production;
- increasingly broad learned knowledge and out-of-distribution generalization;
- reasoning, composition, coding, quantitative problem solving, and synthesis;
- instruction following and stable multi-turn dialogue;
- persistent memory and continual learning without unacceptable catastrophic forgetting;
- internal confidence/risk signals, self-monitoring, and bounded corrective intervention;
- reproducible training, evaluation, rollback, and provenance.

LM-LANE SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE
Characterize which bounded state observables and resource-response laws remain invariant under controlled substrate changes, under fixed resources and preregistered scientific controls. Use those laws to determine how the existing substrate can acquire and sustain increasingly general language-model competence without discarding the substrate.

SCIENTIFIC CONTROLS
Preserve exact negative results. Change one experimental dimension at a time unless a preregistration explicitly defines an interaction study. No post-result threshold, seed, metric, budget, or interpretation retuning. Synthetic and narrow tasks remain valid mechanism probes, but they do not by themselves establish open-domain LLM-class competence.

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
This North Star changes the research destination, not execution authority. No live activation, broker access, accepted-ref mutation, deployment authority, or cross-lane authority is granted by this document.
