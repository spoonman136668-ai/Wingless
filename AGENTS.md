# Wingless operating contract

Wingless is an independent, resource-efficient **cognitive architecture research program**. The current coding/reasoning worker and possible future plane integration are applications/engineering stages, not its scientific destination. Minimize heavy inference and resource use; Codex parity requires empirical evidence.

## Standing cognitive North Star

Develop Wingless' **own native learned cognition**, eventually capable of LLM-class or greater general reasoning/language at lower resource cost if achievable. **Never use a transformer, conventional autoregressive next-token prediction, or token-centric language modeling as its cognitive foundation.** Language input/output and conventional models for controlled baselines, bounded research tools, or teachers are permitted; they must not silently supply the target's cognition. Prioritize persistent representations and memory, continual learning, compositional/causal transfer, adaptation, and integration into a functioning independent system. Yggdrasil remains a separate developmental/regenerative architecture. Synthetic benchmark or throughput gains alone are not North Star progress. See [standing boundary](docs/COGNITIVE_NORTH_STAR.md); the existing hash-pinned active research charter remains unchanged until separately qualified.

Authority: source, tests, deterministic acceptance, then advisory ICE. Model output never grants acceptance or authorization.

Never modify live ckb-plane or CKB product source, production integrations, credentials, Coinbase/broker/runtime state, accepted refs or queue/worker authority. Do not download giant models. Integration is deferred until stabilized plane contracts are supplied.

Query ICE first; read identified symbols/ranges; expand only as evidence requires. Verify consequential claims against source. Rebuild stale ICE. Use small bounded edits, explicit contracts, fail-closed behavior and mock inference. Maintain integration seams and coherent independently reviewable commits.
